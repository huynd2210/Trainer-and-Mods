# Mercenaries (Stoneshard mod)

**Hire a tavern mercenary who travels with you and fights at your side.** Talk to any of the tavern
mercenaries and pick *"Looking for work?"*. Signing costs 500 crowns, then 50 crowns a day, paid
automatically at midnight. If you can't pay, they leave. If they die in your service, they're gone
for good and won't come back to their tavern.

- **Follows you everywhere:** through doors, stairs, area edges, the world map, and save/load.
- **Right-click them** for *Wait here* or *Follow me*. A waiting merc stays where you left them.
- **Left-click them** to swap places, the same as with any friendly NPC.
- They keep their own look and fight the enemies they see. They never turn on you.
- One mercenary at a time.

## Install

Needs [ModShardLauncher](https://github.com/ModShardTeam/ModShardLauncher) (MSL) v0.13.2 and
Stoneshard 0.9.4.24.

1. Extract `Mercenaries.zip` into your ModShardLauncher folder, so that `Mods\Mercenaries.sml`
   lands next to `ModShardLauncher.exe`.
2. Back up the game's `data.win`. Run ModShardLauncher, open `data.win`, tick **Mercenaries**
   and save the patched game.

`Mercenaries-Pack.zip`, if you have it, is MSL with the mod already in `Mods\`. Extract it anywhere
and do step 2.

Works with existing saves. To uninstall, put the original `data.win` back. Uninstalling while a
merc is hired is untested.
