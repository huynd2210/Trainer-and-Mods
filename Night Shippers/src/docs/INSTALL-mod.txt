Night Shippers Trainer - mod only
=================================

This zip is the trainer on its own. It needs UE4SS already installed.

  Already have UE4SS?  Extract this into your Night Shippers folder (the one
                       holding ProjectSH.exe) and start the game.

  Do not have UE4SS?   Use NightShippersTrainer-Pack.zip instead. It contains
                       UE4SS and this mod together, and needs nothing else.

Full notes are in README.txt.
