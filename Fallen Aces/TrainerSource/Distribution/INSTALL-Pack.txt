Fallen Aces Trainer - Pack (includes BepInEx)
=============================================
Self-contained. Includes BepInEx 5.4.23.5 x64, Doorstop 4.5.0 and
Fallen Aces Trainer 1.0.0. This pack contains the Trainer and nothing else.

1. Close Fallen Aces.
2. Extract this ZIP into the folder containing Fallen Aces.exe.
3. BepInEx, winhttp.dll and doorstop_config.ini must sit beside the EXE.
4. Launch normally. Press Insert in a level to open the trainer.

If BepInEx is already installed, use FallenAces.Trainer.zip instead, to
preserve your existing loader and settings. This pack ships a minimal loader
config with its console window disabled; the mod writes its own defaults on
first launch.

Once this pack is installed you can drop other mod-only ZIPs for this game
straight into BepInEx\plugins.

See README.txt for the full cheat list, hotkeys and verification limitations.
Bundled dependencies retain their licenses in licenses/.
