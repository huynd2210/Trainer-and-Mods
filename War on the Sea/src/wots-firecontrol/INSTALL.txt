Fire Control v1.0.0 - Self-Contained Pack
=========================================
This pack bundles BepInEx 5.4.23.2 (x64) plus the WoTSFireControl plugin, so no
separate BepInEx install is needed. It targets the x64 Mono build of
War on the Sea v1.09a.

INSTALL
-------
1. Extract this zip INTO the game folder (the folder that contains
   WarOnTheSea.exe). It drops winhttp.dll, doorstop_config.ini and a
   BepInEx\ folder next to the executable.
2. Launch WarOnTheSea.exe.
3. Start a battle. Your close-in AA stops spraying, and your guns have a full
   fire-control solution from the first director tick instead of walking it up
   over a minute of shooting.

     F8 = toggle Fire Control on/off

   A "FIRE CONTROL" line in the top-left corner shows when it is on.

REMOVE
------
Delete the three items you just added: winhttp.dll, doorstop_config.ini,
and the BepInEx\ folder. Nothing existing is touched.

NOTES
-----
- Player ships only. Enemy AA and enemy directors are untouched.
- NOTHING HOMES. No guided munitions are added anywhere in this mod. Rounds go
  exactly where the mount points them; the point is that it now points correctly.
- Naval shell dispersion is NOT removed - see README.txt. Your guns still
  scatter, they just stop aiming at a guess.
- The two halves have separate Enabled switches in the config, so you can try
  the AA change and the gunnery change one at a time.
- The two AntiAir settings only work together. Tightening the cone without also
  lowering the aim interval makes AA WORSE. README.txt has the measurements.
- Throwing F8 mid-battle updates every player ship afloat, and restores each
  mount's stock settings exactly when switched back off.
- Settings live in BepInEx\config\com.wots.firecontrol.cfg after first run.
- This pack contains only Fire Control. If you also want the War on the Sea
  Trainer, Ace Aviators or More Targets, install any one pack and then drop the
  other mods' plugin DLLs into BepInEx\plugins - they run together and their
  status lines do not overlap.
